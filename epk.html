Bio

Acoustic roots. Pop heart.

Ryan McKenzie is a New England songwriter and live performer whose original music blends acoustic pop and folk. His live sets bring familiar songs from across genres together with his own original material.

Ryan won the FYR360° Community Songwriting Contest for his original song, “Can’t Let You Down.” He plays three-hour acoustic sets at bars and breweries around New England.

What You'll Get

A reliable, low-lift booking

  • Adjustable set lengths and volume to match your schedule.
  • Professional communication before, during, and after the show.
  • Original material plus tasteful covers tailored to the crowd.
  • Compact stage footprint that fits tight corners or small risers.
